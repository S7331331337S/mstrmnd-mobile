# MSTRMND Media System

MSTRMND media should feel like documentation from a sophisticated systems company: quiet, technical, human, and intentional.

## Composition

Use a strict grid, large negative space, compact typography, hairline rules, and a single visual focal point. Favor diagrams, product surfaces, architectural geometry, monochrome photography, material studies, and real work over generic AI imagery.

## Copy hierarchy

**Eyebrow:** 10–12px uppercase, tracked.
**Headline:** 32–56px depending on format.
**Support:** 14–18px, short paragraphs only.
**Footer:** 11–12px metadata / URL.

## Example messages

ORCHESTRATE. AUTOMATE. ELEVATE.

AI SYSTEMS. BUILT FOR WORK.

INTELLIGENCE, WITH INTENT.

BUILD SYSTEMS THAT WORK.

THE OPERATING LAYER FOR YOUR WORK.

## Dark media

Black background, white type, grayscale imagery, subtle silver mark. Border and shadow remain barely visible.

## Light media

White or near-white background, black type, grayscale imagery, charcoal mark. Keep contrast crisp and surfaces quiet.

## Gradient rule

The metallic gradient belongs to the mark and selected premium surfaces only. It should read as reflected light, not as a colorful visual effect.
