# MSTRMND Brand Kit

## Assets

- `assets/mstrmnd-mark.svg` — flat monochrome mark; preferred for UI and small sizes.
- `assets/mstrmnd-mark-metallic.svg` — neutral white/silver gradient; preferred for hero media and premium surfaces.
- `assets/mstrmnd-wordmark.svg` — wordmark source.

## Lockups

Mark and wordmark may be used together when introducing the brand. Preserve generous clear space equal to at least 1/2 the mark height on every side.

## Do

- Use black, white, and grayscale.
- Use the metallic treatment sparingly.
- Keep layouts sparse and typographic.
- Use 1px borders and restrained shadows.
- Produce light and dark variants for every asset.

## Don't

- Add neon, purple, teal, or colored gradients.
- Add heavy bloom or chrome effects.
- Distort, rotate, outline, or decorate the mark.
- Put the mark inside busy photographic compositions.
- Use glow as a substitute for contrast.

## Export guidance

SVG is the source of truth. Export PNG at 1024px, 512px, 256px, 128px, 64px, and 32px where raster assets are required. For app icons, keep the mark optically centered and preserve a quiet black or white field around it.

## Recommended media formats

- Social square: 1080 × 1080
- Social portrait: 1080 × 1350
- Story / reel: 1080 × 1920
- Open Graph: 1200 × 630
- App icon master: 1024 × 1024

## Media formula

01 — mark
02 — one strong statement
03 — one supporting line
04 — restrained footer / URL

Do not fill empty space simply because it is available.
