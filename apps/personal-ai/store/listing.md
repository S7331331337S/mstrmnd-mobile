# Draft product listing

Name: MSTRMND

Subtitle: Your personal AI mastermind

Bring a question, explore different perspectives, and leave with your next move.

MSTRMND gives you a personal board of seven AI specialists and a Chair. Build a table around your question, add your personal context, follow the debate, and turn the Chair's next steps into a checklist.

- Personal context and current focus
- Quick rounds and full debates
- Saved sessions and searchable decisions
- Persistent next-move checklists
- Light, dark, and system appearance
- Scripted demo mode and live Claude using your own key

Live questions, enabled context, and debate transcripts are sent to Anthropic for processing. Saved history and preferences remain on the device.
